FAMU & Sandia: Quantum-AI Synergy

October 2023

Florida A&M University (FAMU) and Sandia National Laboratories have announced a collaborative research initiative aimed at extending the boundaries of deep learning through quantum computing. By leveraging quantum-enhanced algorithms, the partnership seeks to solve complex optimization problems that remain intractable for classical neural networks, paving the way for more efficient model training architectures.

IBM Breakthroughs in Quantum Finance

October 2023

IBM has unveiled significant advancements in machine learning applications for quantum finance. Their latest research demonstrates how quantum-classical hybrid models can outperform traditional deep learning approaches in risk assessment and portfolio optimization. By utilizing quantum kernels, IBM researchers have successfully identified non-linear patterns in volatile market data, offering a new paradigm for financial forecasting.

As the industry moves toward larger parameter counts, the integration of quantum hardware is becoming a focal point for sustainable scaling.
